About Us

We Connect Surplus Food with Hungry Hearts

Every day, tons of perfectly good food goes to waste, while thousands of people go to bed hungry. FoodBridge is here to change that. We bridge the gap between restaurants, donors and those in need, ensuring no good food goes to waste.

Our Mission

Reduce food waste and fight hunger by connecting surplus food with people who need it the most.

Our Vision

A world where no food is wasted and no one sleeps hungry.

Our Values

Compassion, Transparency, Trust, Safety and Community.
