Donation Guidelines

Freshness Matters Only donate food that is safe and within its best-before time.
Pack Properly Use clean, sealed and suitable containers for food.
Mention Storage Let NGOs know if the food needs refrigeration or special care.
Be Accurate Provide the correct quantity, location and pickup timings.

Tip

Adding a clear photo and accurate serving information can help NGOs quickly understand your donation and arrange a pickup.
