How It Works

Simple Steps,
Big Impact

FoodBridge makes it easy for anyone to donate food or get food to those who need it.
Together, we can end food waste and fight hunger.

🏪
RESTAURANT
🚚
🏠
ORPHANAGE
STEP 1

Food is Donated

Restaurants or donors upload details of surplus food available for donation.

STEP 2

Nearby NGOs Notified

Nearby NGOs receive instant notifications about available food.

STEP 3

NGO Accepts

NGO accepts the donation and coordinates pickup details.

STEP 4

Food is Picked Up

Volunteers pick up the food from the donor and ensure safe delivery.

STEP 5

Food Reaches & Impact

Food is delivered to those in need and makes a real difference.

Safe & Verified

All donors and NGOs are verified for trust and safety.

Real-time Tracking

Track your donation in real-time from pickup to delivery.

Reduce Waste

Help reduce food waste and protect our environment.

Create Impact

Every meal you share can bring a smile to someone.
